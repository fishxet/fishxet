### About me

- 🎰 **4+ years of game-dev experience**, **3+ focused specifically on HTML5 slot games** with **Pixi.js** and **TypeScript**.
- 🧱 Built across the full spectrum of slot mechanics — **classic reels & paylines**, **cascade (avalanche)**, **Hold&Win** with sticky symbols and respins, **real-time crash** multiplier games, plus an internal **Slot Core SDK** used as the foundation for new titles.
- ⚙️ Strong focus on **clean architecture**: centralized state machines, **Service Locator / DI**, **pub/sub** messaging, modular design that cleanly separates domain logic from rendering.
- 📱 Performance-minded — maintain **stable 60 FPS on mobile browsers (including iOS Safari)** during heavy animation sequences via batching, texture atlases, and tight control of the render loop.
- 🔌 Deep experience **integrating with backend providers** — replaying server-determined outcomes with pixel-perfect sync between visuals and logic, while keeping the illusion of randomness.
- 🦴 Comfortable with **Spine** runtimes, **WebGL** internals, and the timing/synchronization systems behind slot mechanics.
